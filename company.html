IMPORTANT CLIENT NOTICE

Professional Consultancy & Membership Fee

The non-refundable Professional Consultancy Fee is US$35,000. It is a one-off payment and also serves as the client's Membership Fee to Brinks Group of Companies (Brinks Africa).

The fee covers professional consultancy activities including research and due diligence, negotiation and procurement, transaction management, commission management, and risk management and compliance.

Important payment conditions

  • The US$35,000 fee is non-refundable and payable once.
  • Payment confirmation is required before account activation and onboarding.
  • Shipping, transportation, commodities, insurance and other service charges are separate and invoiced independently.
  • Logistics service invoices are payable 100% upfront before the relevant services commence.

Please review the applicable service agreement and seek clarification from Brinks Logistics & Forwarding Limited before making any payment. This notice is a summary and does not replace the signed agreement.
